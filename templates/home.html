Aleksandr Kovalev

I build AI models for intracortical brain-computer interfaces and run closed-loop experiments with an implanted participant.

PhD student with Eduardo Fernández at Universidad Miguel Hernández, Spain. Our participant is blind and has a Utah array in visual cortex. I write the stimulation software, run the sessions and build the decoders.

Before that I co-founded ALVI Labs: our model decoded the movement of every finger from forearm EMG in real time, and people with amputations used it to move the fingers of a virtual hand.

Also: 3rd place in the NeurIPS 2025 LibriBrain competition (speech detection from MEG). Physics at Moscow State University, cognitive neuroscience at HSE, computer vision engineer before BCI.
