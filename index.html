Aleksandr Kovalev

I build AI models for intracortical brain-computer interfaces.

I am a PhD student in Eduardo Fernández's lab at Universidad Miguel Hernández, Spain. I run closed-loop experiments with a blind participant who has a Utah array implanted in primary visual cortex: stimulation software, protocols, sessions and analysis. I work on self-supervised models of neural data, real-time decoding and models of cortical stimulation.

Before that I co-founded ALVI Labs, where we built EMG control for hand prostheses, and worked as a computer vision engineer. With the ALVI team we took 3rd place in LibriBrain at NeurIPS 2025, speech detection from MEG. I studied physics at Moscow State University and cognitive neuroscience at HSE.

I am looking for research internships in BCI for 2027.
